# Privacy Policy

## Cookies & Tracking
As part of this website, I do not use cookies of any form, nor do I collect any data for the purposes of cross-site tracking, personal identification, or monetary gain.

## Analytics
This website uses a self-hosted instance of **[Umami](https://umami.is/)**, an open-source, privacy-focused web analytics tool, to collect anonymous usage statistics (such as page views, referrers, device/browser types, and country). Umami is completely cookie-free, does not collect any personally identifiable information (PII), and does not track users across websites. Because it is self-hosted, all analytics data remains under my control and is never shared with third parties.

## Data Collection & Storage
Your public IP address is collected by my database and API for the purposes of:
- Serving data to you
- Debugging the source of an error if a problem arises
- Preventing abuse of the website and its services

This data is never shared with any third parties, is used solely for the purposes of maintaining the website and its services, and is only retained for a period of 5 days.

## Third-Party Services
The only third-party services in use are:
- **[Cloudflare](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/)** (DNS & Captcha)
- **[GitHub](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-privacy-statement)** (Web hosting)

These third-party services have their own privacy policies; please consult them for more information.
