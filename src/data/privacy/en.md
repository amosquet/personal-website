# Privacy Policy

## Cookies & Tracking
As part of this website, I do not use cookies of any form, nor do I collect any data for the purposes of tracking, identification, or monetary gain.

## Data Collection & Storage
Your public IP address is collected by my database and API for the purposes of:
- Serving data to you
- Debugging the source of an error if a problem arises
- Preventing abuse of the website and its services

This data is never shared with any third parties, is used solely for the purposes of maintaining the website and its services, and is only retained for a period of 5 days.

## Third-Party Services
The only third-party services in use are:
- **[Cloudflare](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/)** (DNS & Captcha)
- **[GitHub](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-privacy-statement)** (Web hosting)

These third-party services have their own privacy policies; please consult them for more information.
